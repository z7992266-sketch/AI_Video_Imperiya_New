Privacy Policy

B2B SAS Channel Manager · Developer: B2B SAS (b2bsasagency.com) · Effective 21 November 2026

This application uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Who can use the app

The app is an internal tool. Only the owner of B2B SAS connects his own Google accounts and YouTube channels. No other users sign in.

Data we access

  • Basic Google account info: email address (to know which connected account a channel belongs to).
  • YouTube channel data of the connected channels: channel id and title, list of our videos, public comments on our videos, view and watch-time statistics.

How we use it

  • To upload our own videos and set their thumbnails.
  • To read and reply to comments on our own videos.
  • To view our channels' statistics for content planning.

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with third parties, do not use it for advertising, and do not use it to train AI models. Comment texts are processed only to write a reply and are not stored after the reply is posted.

Storage and security

OAuth refresh tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest in a private database (Supabase) accessible only to the app's server functions. Access tokens are short-lived and are not stored. We delete stored tokens within 30 days after a channel is disconnected or on request.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or ask us to delete your data by email.

Limited Use

B2B SAS Channel Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
