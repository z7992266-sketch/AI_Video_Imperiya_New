B2B SAS Channel Manager

An internal tool of B2B SAS (b2bsasagency.com), Uzbekistan.

B2B SAS Channel Manager is a private, internal application that B2B SAS uses to manage its own YouTube channels (Taqqos_Uz, CineMatrix AI, OUJ_Official, GLITCHVERSE, Exclusive Vibe and The Laura Files). It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's channel.

What the app does

  • Uploads videos that our team produced to our own channels, with titles, descriptions and thumbnails (YouTube Data API: videos.insert, thumbnails.set).
  • Replies to viewer comments on our own videos (commentThreads.list, comments.insert).
  • Reads channel statistics (views, watch time) to plan future content (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).

How Google user data is used

The app only accesses YouTube data of the channels that the channel owner connected via Google sign-in. The data is used solely for the functions above, is never sold or shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
